<objective>
## Goal
Enhance the gallery experience with photo metadata display in the lightbox and "Load More" pagination for the gallery, preparing the site to gracefully handle a growing photo collection.

## Purpose
The photo data structure now has rich metadata (description, tags, date) from Plan 02-01, but none of it is displayed to visitors. Additionally, with 6 photos all rendered at once, the gallery needs pagination infrastructure before the collection grows to dozens or hundreds of photos.

## Output
- Lightbox shows photo metadata (date, description, tags) in the bottom info bar
- Gallery paginates with a "Load More" button when photos exceed a configurable threshold
- Both enhancements match the existing dark cinematic brutalism design system
</objective>

